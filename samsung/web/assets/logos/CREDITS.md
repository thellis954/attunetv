# Installed-app identification artwork

These marks identify apps already present in the TV inventory. They do not add apps, change launch IDs, imply affiliation, or indicate playback integration. Files are bundled offline; no logo CDN receives viewing/app requests. Trademarks remain with their owners.

Retrieved November 21, 2026:

- `netflix.svg`: Netflix wordmark, unmodified SVG from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Netflix_2015_logo.svg (source Netflix; Commons marks PD-textlogo).
- `prime-video.svg`: Amazon's historical Prime Video wordmark, unmodified SVG from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amazon_Prime_Video_logo.svg (Commons marks PD-textlogo).
- `hulu.svg`: Hulu wordmark, unmodified SVG from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hulu_logo_(2018).svg (source Hulu press assets; Commons marks PD-textlogo).
- `disney-plus.svg`: Disney+ mark, unmodified SVG from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_logo.svg .
- `apple-tv.svg`, `youtube.svg`: Simple Icons 16.38.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.38.0/icons . Explicit white/red fill added; paths unchanged. Project license: CC0-1.0; trademark and individual brand restrictions remain. https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.38.0/LICENSE.md and https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.38.0/DISCLAIMER.md .

Other icons are read from installed app resources on the user's TV and are not redistributed in this package. Some OS-supplied icons contain baked-in borders or padding that cannot be removed without cropping the artwork.
