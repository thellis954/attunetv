# AttuneTV 0.5.1 — your phone, your viewing companion

The public Android APK now includes **Ask → Pair phone**: scan the QR code, approve your phone, connect OpenAI, Claude, Grok or Kimi through guided API-key setup, and keep a conversation about the title, episode and progress you enter. API usage is billed by your provider; an existing consumer chat subscription does not cover it.

## Choose your download

- **AttuneTV-Setup.zip:** recommended for new users. Includes the signed APK, configured unsigned Samsung WGT, Mac/Linux and Windows Android installers, checksums and all setup guides. Extract and open START-HERE.txt. Google Platform-Tools is required for Android installation.
- **AttuneTV.apk:** Android/Fire TV 0.5.1, versionCode 12. Same official signing identity as 0.4.2 for updates that preserve settings.
- **AttuneTV-Samsung-UNSIGNED.wgt:** Samsung client 0.1.1, configured for the hosted companion. Requires your own Samsung TV certificate and installation permission; it is not directly installable or a Samsung Store listing. No source build or GitHub login needed.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt**, **release-info.json**, **samsung-release-info.json:** checksums, source commit, versions and signing/validation information.

## What's included

- Shared viewing context and conversation between TV and phone; phone chat can continue when the TV app closes.
- Guided provider account, billing, key and model setup on the phone; optional browser dictation with transcript review.
- Updated cinematic Fire TV Home, search and app organization from the current source.
- Samsung Tizen 4 packaged-origin compatibility in the hosted service.
- Guided Android installers that verify checksum and installed version, stop on authorization/signature errors and never automatically uninstall, reset settings or change Home behavior.

## Preview limits and verification

Android build, tests and lint plus companion service/PostgreSQL/browser/package checks gate publication. Browser answers use fixtures. Historical physical checks cover the Insignia launcher and Samsung 0.1.0 installation/QR generation; this new release is not claimed to have completed all physical-device or real-provider acceptance tests.

Samsung is full-screen TV chat plus phone chat during playback, not an overlay or Smart Hub replacement. Fire TV streaming overlays remain hidden until each app passes real playback/focus/dismissal tests. Android TV/Google TV compatibility is experimental. No native Roku, Apple TV hardware, LG webOS or non-Android Fire TV package is included.

Context is supplied by you; AttuneTV does not see/hear the video or read subtitles. Answers and spoiler avoidance are not guaranteed. Development APKs may use a different signer; stop on a mismatch and review the update guide rather than erasing data. No always-on installer computer is required.
